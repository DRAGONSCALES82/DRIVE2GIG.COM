Know the rental rules before you drive

Passenger rideshare eligibility comes first

Lyft requires Arizona rental vehicles to come through Express Drive. Uber recommends its approved rental partners through the Vehicle Marketplace. A qualifying model year alone does not guarantee approval. Do not rent a DRIVE2GIG vehicle for passenger rides unless the platform, rental provider and insurer confirm that specific rental is permitted.

Rideshare rental FAQs

Is DRIVE2GIG a Lyft Express Drive partner?

No affiliation is claimed. Arizona Lyft rentals must be through Express Drive.

Are these cars automatically Uber-approved?

No. Verify the specific rental provider and vehicle with Uber before signing.

What if I also deliver food or groceries?

Ask about permitted delivery use and appropriate insurance; requirements vary by app.
