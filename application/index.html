Step 5 of 6

Read and acknowledge agreement

Full rental terms from the revised Drive2Gig agreement are provided below. Scroll to review them.

Drive2Gig is the business name of the legal Owner entered on page 1. "Vehicle" includes keys, equipment, and accessories. "Renter" means the named customer.

1. Minimum term and extensions. The initial rental commitment is at least 30 consecutive 24-hour days from the start date shown above. Any early return does not automatically waive unpaid, expressly agreed minimum-term rent, subject to applicable law. There is no automatic extension unless recorded in a signed Schedule. Continued possession without written approval is not a renewal.

2. Payment in full or vehicle returned. Rent and other scheduled amounts are payable in advance by the due date and time on page 1. Owner must actually receive the full required payment; a promise, screenshot, pending transfer, or partial payment does not qualify. If the payment is not received in full on time, Owner may suspend use, end the rental, and demand prompt return as permitted by law. Renter must safely stop operating and follow the return instructions. A partial payment does not give permission to keep driving. Any outstanding amount remains due; no illegal penalty is imposed.

3. Security deposit and additional charges. The deposit may be applied to unpaid rent, damage, fuel, tolls, citations, cleaning, towing, and agreed fees, but is not a limit on lawful responsibility. Owner will return any remaining deposit after reasonable final reconciliation, subject to disclosed and legal reserves for delayed claims. Each charge must be supported; Owner will credit insurance payments for the same loss and will not collect twice.

4. Renter must provide liability insurance. Before pickup and throughout possession, Renter must obtain and pay for valid insurance that actually covers the named driver, the rented/non-owned Vehicle, personal/off-app use, and every approved rideshare/delivery phase. Unless a signed Schedule requires more, off-app coverage must provide at least $100,000 bodily injury per person / $300,000 per accident and $100,000 property damage, or $300,000 combined single limit. When an app is on, waiting, en route, or carrying passengers/deliveries, coverage must satisfy current law and Platform rules with no gaps. Acceptable coverage may combine verified Renter and Platform policies. An insurance card or standard personal policy alone does not prove the activity is insured.

5. Proof, verification, and loss of coverage. Renter must provide declarations, policy dates, listed drivers/vehicle or non-owned coverage, endorsements, and proof for approved Platforms before pickup and on request. Renter authorizes verification from insurers, brokers, and Platforms, including daily status checks. Renter must immediately disclose a cancellation, missed premium, exclusion, policy change, or loss of coverage. If required coverage lapses or cannot be verified, permission to drive is suspended; a confirmed lapse is grounds for immediate termination and a lawful return demand. Do not operate until Owner confirms authorization.

6. Driver liability and vehicle damage. The driver is personally responsible for their negligent, reckless, unlawful, or otherwise legally actionable driving and resulting third-party claims to the extent imposed by law. Separately, Renter is responsible for new Vehicle damage, theft or loss, deductibles, and reasonably documented related expenses arising during possession to the extent permitted by law and the insurance contract. Liability insurance for third parties is NOT the same as collision/comprehensive coverage for Drive2Gig's Vehicle. Renter is not automatically legally at fault merely because a crash happened. Owner negligence, nonwaivable legal duties, applicable insurer payments, and third-party responsibility remain subject to law.

7. Approved drivers and approved work. Only Renter and any driver approved in writing by Owner and accepted by the applicable insurer may drive. No lending, subrental, sharing, unauthorized passengers as drivers, or transfer of keys. Authorized use is lawful personal driving and the specifically approved Platforms and activities listed on the Schedule. Platform account approval and insurance approval are Renter's responsibility. Owner does not guarantee Platform acceptance, trips, earnings, or replacement vehicles.

8. Accidents, theft, and new damage. Stop safely, call 911 when needed, notify police when legally required, and notify Drive2Gig immediately at 602-471-4743 once safe (normally no later than 1 hour after the scene is safe). Notify insurers and the Platform, collect photos, report number, witnesses, and trip/app timestamps; send a written incident report within 24 hours. Do not settle, admit legal liability, or authorize non-emergency repairs without Owner consent. Do not drive after an accident or suspected unsafe damage until Owner authorizes it in writing. Cooperate with all claims inquiries.

9. Maintenance and safe operation. Owner covers reasonable scheduled maintenance and normal-wear mechanical repairs. Renter must check tires, lights, leaks, and warning indicators; immediately stop safely for overheating, oil pressure, brakes/steering failure, serious tire damage, fluid loss, burning smell, or other unsafe condition; and report it promptly. Renter must present the Vehicle for approved service or inspection when directed and may not order non-emergency repairs or towing without written approval. Renter pays loss reasonably caused by misuse, impact, improper fuel, neglect, missed service, or continued driving after warning, to the extent permitted by law.

10. Cleanliness, fuel, and conduct. No smoking, vaping, cannabis, burning materials, smoke contamination, reckless or impaired driving, racing, off-road driving, towing, illegal uses, unauthorized modifications, or travel outside approved territory. Keep the interior sanitary and remove trash. Return gas/hybrid Vehicles with a full tank unless the Schedule states a different return level, and EVs at the scheduled charge level. Reasonable documented cleaning, remediation, refueling, and related agreed charges may apply; ordinary wear is not chargeable.

11. Tolls, tickets, and limits. Renter pays tolls, parking fees, traffic violations, authorized mileage overages, impound, storage, and expenses attributable to Renter's possession or use. Renter must notify Owner of any towing, impound, seizure, damage, or police matter. Mileage and territorial restrictions are stated on the Schedule. Late-received tolls and citations may be charged with fees specified in the Fee Schedule.

12. GPS, inspections, and records. Vehicles may have GPS/telematics recording location, mileage, diagnostic, and driving events for vehicle safety, insurance, maintenance, billing, and lawful recovery. Do not tamper with the devices. Owner may request vehicle photographs or inspections for reasonable operational reasons. Driver documentation, insurance checks, photos, communications, and payment records may be kept and shared with service providers, insurers, law enforcement, or counsel when lawful and reasonably necessary.

13. Default, termination, and recovery. Nonpayment, no qualifying insurance, unauthorized driver or Platform, major damage or unsafe use, fraud, smoking, serious maintenance refusal, tracking tampering, or refusal to return is a material breach. Owner may terminate or suspend permission where permitted by law and demand the Vehicle, keys, and equipment. Renter must safely return the Vehicle by the lawful deadline and location Owner directs. Owner may use only lawful notice, towing, repossession, or court procedures and shall not breach the peace. Reasonable documented lawful recovery expenses may be owed by Renter.

14. Return and final accounting. Return only at the agreed place and time, with keys and equipment. The rental ends when Owner accepts return or legally recovers the Vehicle, not merely when Renter stops driving. Condition may be reviewed after return for concealed damage and delayed tolls or claims. A preliminary receipt is not a waiver of lawful charges. Left-behind personal property will be handled as required by law.

15. General legal terms. Arizona law governs; disputes are heard in a court of competent jurisdiction in Maricopa County unless law requires otherwise. Each party should give written notice of a nonurgent dispute and allow 10 calendar days for a response before filing suit; urgent legal action is unaffected. The prevailing party may seek fees only where authorized by law. This Agreement, completed schedules, and later signed changes constitute the entire agreement. Invalid provisions do not invalidate the rest. Electronic signatures and delivery are allowed. Nothing waives nonwaivable rights or statutory duties, including Owner insurance duties under A.R.S. § 28-2166.
